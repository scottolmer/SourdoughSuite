### Pre-Launch
- [ ] Finalize app name/subtitle

- [ ] Write full description

## ⭐ Review Strategy

### When to Ask
- After 3rd bake logged
- After using 5 features
- 2 weeks after download
- Never more than 3x/year

### Prompt
```
"Enjoying Sourdough Suite? Your feedback
helps other bakers discover the app.
Would you mind rating us?"

[Rate Now] [Maybe Later] [No Thanks]
```

### Response Time
- Reply to 100% within 48 hours
- Thank 5-star reviews
- Fix issues from 1-3 star reviews
- Update responses when bugs fixed

## 🚨 Risk Mitigation

### Challenge: Low Downloads
- **Fix:** Search ads, influencer outreach, email users

### Challenge: Poor Conversion (<35%)
- **Fix:** A/B test screenshots, redesign first impression

### Challenge: Bad Reviews
- **Fix:** Rapid response, quick bug fixes, better onboarding

### Challenge: Keyword Rankings
- **Fix:** Refresh keywords, content marketing, paid ads
