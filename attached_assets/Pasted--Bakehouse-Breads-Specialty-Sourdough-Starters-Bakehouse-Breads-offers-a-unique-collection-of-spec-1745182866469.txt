# Bakehouse Breads Specialty Sourdough Starters

Bakehouse Breads offers a unique collection of specialty sourdough starters, each with distinct characteristics, flavor profiles, and baking properties. Our starters have been carefully cultivated to provide home bakers with exceptional results and exciting flavor possibilities.

## Koji Sourdough Starter
**$24.95**

### Overview
Our Koji sourdough starter is a Japanese-inspired culture featuring exceptional enzymatic activity that creates bread with distinctively sweet and complex flavors. This unique starter produces loaves with subtle umami notes and an exceptionally tender crumb structure.

### Key Features
- Natural sweetness with complex umami undertones
- Exceptional enzymatic activity for efficient carbohydrate breakdown
- Creates bread with a tender, moist crumb structure
- Performs best in slightly warmer environments (75-80°F)
- Ideal for enriched doughs and Asian-inspired breads

### Care Requirements
- Feed daily with a 1:1:1 ratio (starter:flour:water)
- Maintain at a slightly warmer temperature than other starters
- Benefits from 10% whole wheat flour in feeding mix
- Use within 4-8 hours of feeding for optimal results

### Recommended Recipes
- Toasted Rice and Seaweed Loaf
- Maple Bourbon Pecan Bread
- Koji-Marinated Apple Walnut Bread
- Miso Butternut Squash Bread
- Koji Amazake Sweet Bread

## San Francisco Style Sourdough Starter
**$19.95**

### Overview
Our San Francisco style starter provides the classic tangy flavor profile that made sourdough famous. Developed with the traditional microbial balance featuring Lactobacillus sanfranciscensis, this starter creates bread with perfect acidity and depth of flavor.

### Key Features
- Traditional tangy flavor profile with excellent balance
- Robust, predictable fermentation characteristics
- Classic sourdough character with perfect acidity
- Performs well across a wide range of hydrations
- Creates excellent open crumb structure with crisp crust

### Care Requirements
- Feed every 12-24 hours at room temperature
- Standard 1:2:2 ratio (starter:flour:water) works well
- Prefers slightly cooler temperatures (65-72°F)
- Can be refrigerated for up to 2 weeks between feedings

### Recommended Recipes
- Classic San Francisco Sourdough Boule
- Sprouted Grain Porridge Loaf
- Fermented Black Garlic Sourdough
- San Francisco Style Sourdough Baguettes
- Traditional Sourdough Bread Bowls

## Kombucha Sourdough Starter
**$18.00**

### Overview
Our Kombucha sourdough starter is infused with tea cultures that create bread with bright, complex acidity and subtle fruity notes. This innovative starter brings unique flavor dimensions to your baking with a mild tanginess different from traditional sourdough.

### Key Features
- Unique tea-infused culture with bright, complex acidity
- Subtle fruity notes and aromatic qualities
- Excellent for whole grain and fruited breads
- Milder tang than San Francisco but more complex flavor profile
- Creates bread with excellent keeping qualities

### Care Requirements
- Feed every 12 hours for best results
- Use 1:3:3 ratio (starter:flour:water) for optimal activity
- Room temperature (68-75°F) is ideal
- Benefits from occasional green tea addition (10% of water weight)

### Recommended Recipes
- Kombucha Sourdough English Muffins
- Tangy Kombucha Discard Pancakes
- Kombucha Sourdough Cinnamon Rolls
- Rustic Kombucha Country Loaf with Dried Fruit
- Cherry Matcha Swirl Bread

## Extra Sour Starter
**Price varies**

### Overview
Our Extra Sour starter is developed specifically for those who crave intense tanginess in their sourdough. This specialized culture produces bread with a pronounced acidic flavor profile, perfect for traditional European-style breads and rye loaves.

### Key Features
- Intensely tangy and acidic flavor profile
- Creates classic rustic sourdough with pronounced flavors
- Excellent for rye and whole grain breads
- Provides characteristic sourness that stands up to robust fillings
- Develops exceptional flavor complexity over long fermentations

### Care Requirements
- Feed once daily with 1:5:5 ratio (starter:flour:water)
- Benefits from cooler fermentation temperatures (62-68°F)
- Performs best with 20-30% rye or whole wheat in feeding
- Allow to fully mature between feedings (12-24 hours)

### Recommended Recipes
- Extra Sour Rye Bread with Caraway
- Roasted Beet and Dill Rye
- Chocolate Cherry Sourdough Rye
- Alpine Mountain Bread with Smoked Cheese
- Nordic Fig and Fennel Rye
