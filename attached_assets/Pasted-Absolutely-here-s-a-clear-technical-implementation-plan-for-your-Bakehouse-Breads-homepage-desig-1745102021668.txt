8. Styling Notes
	•	Use Tailwind CSS for utility-first layout and styling.
	•	Use font-serif for Lora/Merriweather (headings) and font-sans (like Inter) for body text.
	•	Responsive-first design: stack sections vertically, use sm: or md: breakpoints for enhancement.
	•	Use .max-w-screen-md mx-auto for content width control on larger screens.

⸻

9. Fonts & Colors Reference

Typography:
	•	Headings: font-serif (Lora or Merriweather)
	•	Body: font-sans (Inter, Open Sans, or Roboto)

Colors:
	•	Background: bg-stone-50 or bg-white
	•	Primary (Amber): bg-amber-600 / text-amber-800
	•	Neutral Text: text-stone-700, text-stone-900
